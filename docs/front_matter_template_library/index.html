    Suggested Working Process

    1. Pick the template that matches the post type.
    2. Fill in the title and excerpt first.
    3. Choose 1 - 3 categories maximum.
    4. Choose 4 - 8 tags maximum.
    5. Keep TOC on for long-form posts and off for short updates.
    6. Reuse consistent labels and icons by section so the site feels designed, not random.

    Final Note

    This library is meant to make your writing workflow faster and more consistent.

    Over time, we’ll expand it into:

    • collection-specific templates
    • post checklists
    • SEO title / excerpt patterns
    • standardized callout blocks
    • reusable project-page shells
