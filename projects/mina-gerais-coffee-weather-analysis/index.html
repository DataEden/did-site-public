    Analytical Workflow

    How the Coffee Production Weather Analysis Works

    The project combines historical agricultural production data with seasonal weather observations to investigate whether changes in coffee output are more closely associated with climate variation or expanding production capacity.

    🌱 Production Data

    Agricultural Output

    Historical coffee production statistics provide the foundation for measuring changes in agricultural output over time.

    The production series is used to identify longer-term trends, periods of growth or contraction, and changes that may reflect shifts in agricultural capacity.

    🌦️ Weather Data

    Seasonal Climate Signals

    Weather observations provide the environmental layer of the analysis, including variables such as rainfall and temperature.

    These measurements are used to examine whether seasonal climate conditions correspond with changes observed in coffee production.

    🔗 Data Integration

    Aligned Analytical Series

    Agricultural and weather datasets are prepared and aligned so their patterns can be compared across a common analytical timeline.

    Derived outputs support exploration of relationships among production levels, weather conditions, and broader changes in the agricultural system.

    📊 Interpretation

    Climate vs. Capacity

    The central analytical question is whether coffee production growth appears to be explained primarily by short-term climate variation or by broader changes in productive capacity.

    Visual and statistical comparisons are used to separate simple correlation from a more useful interpretation of the factors associated with changing production levels.

    Data Foundation

    Agricultural and Weather Sources

    The analysis combines historical coffee production statistics from Brazilian agricultural reporting sources with seasonal weather observations, including rainfall and temperature measurements.

    🔎 Data Source

    Supporting datasets and derived analytical outputs are stored within the project directory so the analytical workflow remains reviewable alongside the project documentation.

    The combined dataset provides the basis for comparing production trends with seasonal environmental conditions and investigating whether observed changes are consistent with climate effects, capacity expansion, or a combination of both.
