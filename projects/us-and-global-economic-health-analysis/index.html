    Analytical Workflow

    How the Economic Health Analysis Works

    The project combines multiple public economic indicators into a broader analytical view of household and macroeconomic conditions, allowing income, cost pressures, and liquidity trends to be examined together rather than as isolated measurements.

    🏦 Public Indicators

    Macroeconomic Data Foundation

    The analysis integrates public economic indicators from sources such as the U.S. Federal Reserve Economic Data system (FRED), the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), and related macroeconomic datasets.

    These sources provide the underlying measurements used to study changes in economic conditions over time.

    🔗 Data Integration

    Multiple Economic Series

    Economic indicators are brought together into a common analytical workflow so that trends in income, living costs, and liquidity can be compared across related periods.

    Supporting datasets and derived outputs provide a reviewable connection between the original indicators and the resulting analysis.

    📈 Trend Analysis

    Income, Costs, and Liquidity

    The analytical layer examines how major economic measures change over time and how those movements relate to broader measures of economic health.

    Rather than relying on a single headline statistic, the project compares multiple signals to build a more complete view of changing economic conditions.

    🌍 Interpretation

    Economic Conditions in Context

    The combined indicators are interpreted as related signals of economic well-being, purchasing pressure, and financial capacity.

    The project uses these relationships to communicate a broader economic story across U.S. conditions and the global context represented within the underlying datasets.
